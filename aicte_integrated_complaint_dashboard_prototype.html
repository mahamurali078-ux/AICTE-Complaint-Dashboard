Configuration

Prototype assumptions and suggested production controls.

Recommended production configuration

  • Configure official AICTE bureaus, cells and officer roles.
  • Define complaint categories, priority rules and SLA timelines.
  • Enable secure authentication, role-based access and audit logs.
  • Set up approved email / notification and escalation workflows.
  • Apply data retention, privacy and document access policies.
  • Connect to authorized complaint intake systems and verified data sources.

This standalone prototype does not authenticate users, send notifications, or connect to official AICTE systems.
